available under the [MIT License](LICENSE), which permits use, modification,
distribution, and commercial use subject to the license notice and disclaimer.

## Contributions

Contributions are welcome. By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may
be distributed under the project's MIT License.

Keep changes focused, include checks for behavioral changes, and do not commit
Zoom credentials, media, transcripts, or other customer data.

## Security

See [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md) for the credential and data-handling
model. Please do not include secrets or private media in public issue reports.
